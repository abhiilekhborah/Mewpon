# Mewpon demo video guide

Lead with visible results. A two- to three-minute video can show the core experience, followed by a clearly labeled preview of voice and meeting tools.

## Suggested sequence

| Moment | What to show | Why it works on video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Summon the companion | Type `mew`, press Command, hide it, then summon it again. If the launcher is configured, show reopening after Quit. | Establishes the distinctive interaction immediately. |
| 2. Act on the Mac | Enter `Open Safari`, then `Change to dark mode`. | Shows natural language producing visible native results. |
| 3. Combine tasks | Try `Open Safari and set volume to 30%` after rehearsing the exact request. | Demonstrates supported sequential actions in one instruction. |
| 4. Find something by purpose | Add a demo folder containing a sample resume or project brief, then search for it and open the result. | Shows useful retrieval without exposing personal files. |
| 5. Arrange a workspace | Ask Mewpon to move Safari to the left half of the screen. | Gives the viewer an immediate before/after. Exact placement depends on the app's minimum size and Accessibility support. |
| 6. Make the companion feel alive | Open Play Mode, pet it, request a high five, then switch an outfit or activity. | Shows interaction and character beyond a chat box. |
| 7. Switch the look | Cycle through two or three themes with a conversation already visible. | Shows the visual design and preserved conversation/avatar state. |
| 8. Show local AI | Ask a short explanatory question and show streaming text. Briefly identify the installed local model. | Makes the local intelligence easy to understand. |
| 9. Voice preview | Rehearse one short English voice command; show Voice Studio playback, moving avatar mouth, and Stop. | Connects recognition, voice output, and animation. Live human recognition still needs rehearsal. |
| 10. Meeting preview | Import a short staged recording, inspect the transcript, generate extractive notes, jump to supporting text, then export Markdown or PDF. | Demonstrates an end-to-end use case. Use the import path for the current demo; system-audio capture remains unverified. |

For a short teaser, use moments 1, 2, 4, 6, and 7. For a technical demo, add the native-action result ledger, model settings, and meeting evidence links.

## Optional extras

- Create a local reminder for a future time and show it in the reminder list. Notification delivery requires a separate successful rehearsal.
- Show a saved routine already configured with supported steps.
- Convert a unit or query another time zone to show a useful response without inference.
- Show opt-in weather with its source and timestamp if internet use fits the story.
- Show offline speaker clusters on a rehearsed staged recording. Explain that Speaker 1/2 are anonymous clusters, not identified people.

## Before recording

1. Keep this guide as preparation; it does not launch or foreground any app.
2. Prepare sample files and a short consented/staged meeting recording. Hide personal notifications and account details.
3. Check the required existing permissions and action policies. Show one meaningful approval rather than implying permission checks do not exist.
4. Rehearse the exact phrases, especially multi-step requests and window arrangement. Restore volume and appearance afterward.
5. Warm the selected local model before a polished recording; describe the clip as a warm session if discussing speed. Do not present edited wait time as measured latency.
6. Label newer features as preview. Avoid promising universal app control, flawless Hindi recognition, automatic identity recognition, live sports scores, or verified system-audio capture.

Suggested opening caption: **“Meet Mewpon: a local AI companion that turns everyday requests into native Mac actions.”**

Suggested closing caption: **“Built for macOS. Local AI, native tools, and a companion with personality.”**
